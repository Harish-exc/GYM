Sunday — Rest Day

No lifting. Just movement and stretching to help your body recover.

  1. Walking — 20 to 30 minutes, easy pace
  2. Stretching: chest, back, shoulders, legs — 30 sec each, 2 rounds
  3. Optional: light mobility (arm swings, hip circles, neck rolls)
Simple is sustainable. If any exercise feels too hard, drop the weight or skip it — consistency matters more than intensity.
